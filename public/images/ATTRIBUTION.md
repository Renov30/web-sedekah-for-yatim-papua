### Child Safeguarding & Visual Dignity Policy
Before replacing any asset:
1. Ensure explicit written/verbal consent from parents or guardians.
2. Avoid any imagery depicting children in distress, disheveled state, or exploitative emotional manipulation.
3. Obscure sensitive personal identifying documents or addresses.
